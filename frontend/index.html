Our Story

Rooted in soil,
pressed by hand.

We partner with named farms and press each batch the slow way — wood-ghani, stone-turned, no heat added. It takes longer. It tastes like it should.

2018Started blending botanical oils in a farmhouse kitchen.
2020Opened a countryside pressing studio with three artisans.
2022Earned organic & cruelty-free certification for our core range.
2024Launched a returnable-glass refill program with 40 retail partners.
From Farm to Bottle

Four steps. No shortcuts.

01

Harvest

Coconuts hand-picked at peak ripeness from partner farms, milled within 24 hours.

02

Wood-Press

Chekku/ghani wheel turns slowly, keeping the oil below body temperature.

03

Settle & Filter

Gravity-settled and cloth-filtered only — no centrifuge, no bleaching.

04

Bottle

Hand-poured into reusable glass, labelled and sealed in small batches.
